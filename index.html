Why “Oracle”?

Streaming algorithms have always decided who gets heard and who doesn't, the same way record labels and radio programmers did a century ago. The name is a nod to the oracles people once traveled to for answers they couldn't find on their own. Type in an artist, and Sonic Oracle hands you the music the algorithm was never going to play for you.
