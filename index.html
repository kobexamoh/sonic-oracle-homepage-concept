Sonic Oracle homepage concepts

Nine versions of the homepage, oldest to newest. Open any of them live, or skim the full-page screenshots. When you reply, name the version and the specific thing you want carried forward: a font, a section, a color, a line of copy.

Short on time? Start with v7. It is the latest, and it has a font picker in the top bar.

The versions

  1. v1

    Sep 9

    First pass, dark

    Dark background, gold accent, Fraunces headings and Inter body text. Hero with a sample playlist card, a comparison against the usual algorithm, three steps, a testimonial, and a closing call to action.

    Open v1 live Full-page screenshot

  2. v1.5

    Sep 14

    Same layout, light palette

    The v1 layout with the colors flipped to a warm off-white. Nothing else changed, which makes it the cleanest light-versus-dark comparison.

    Open v1.5 live Full-page screenshot

  3. v2

    Sep 14

    Light and dark toggle

    Adds a toggle that follows the visitor's device setting by default. The gradients and glows are gone from the cards, and the vinyl graphic is a plain outline.

    Open v2 live Light screenshot Dark screenshot

  4. v3

    Sep 15

    New copy, new fonts, new section

    Rewrote the copy to cut phrases that sound machine-written. Fonts move to Playfair Display and DM Sans, which match the live app. Adds a "Why Oracle?" section and a second line under the difference heading that fades in as you scroll. The Tidal, Qobuz, and YouTube Music chips are now real links.

    Open v3 live Light screenshot Dark screenshot

  5. v4

    Sep 15

    Scroll animation

    Every section fades in once as you scroll down and stays put when you scroll back up. The platform chips carry a faint brand color that goes full strength on hover. Also fixes the alignment of the second difference line.

    Open v4 live Light screenshot Dark screenshot

  6. v4.5

    Sep 16

    Light only, bolder type, navy accent

    Dark mode is paused while the light theme gets tuned. Headings and body text are heavier. A navy accent from sonicoracle.music shows up on the step numbers and nav hover. The difference cards are flat, and the page uses the live site's favicon.

    Open v4.5 live Full-page screenshot

  7. v5

    Sep 17

    Pricing section

    Adds the pricing section earlier versions lacked: Lifetime at $39.99 one time and Yearly at $14.99, taken from the live site. The Pricing link in the nav now lands on it.

    Open v5 live Full-page screenshot

  8. v6

    Sep 19

    Readability pass

    Body text goes from 16px to 17px. The small uppercase labels above the hero headline and the pricing heading are gone. Heading line spacing is now consistent, which fixes a cramped hero headline and loose section headings.

    Open v6 live Full-page screenshot

  9. v7

    LatestSep 19

    Font picker

    A dropdown in the top bar swaps the heading font live, with eight alternatives to Playfair Display. Your pick is remembered if you refresh. Scheherazade New is an Arabic-first typeface, so its Latin letters look odd here.

    Open v7 live Full-page screenshot
